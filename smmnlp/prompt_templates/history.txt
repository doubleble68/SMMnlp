
Your are given a clinical note that explicitly mentions smoldering multiple myeloma (SMM). Your task is to determine whether the mentioned SMM diagnosis is historical or new.

### ABSTRACTION RULES
Output "hx" only when the note has the following evidence for historical diagnosis:
    - SMM listed under medical history, oncology history, problem list, or history of present illness (HPI)
    - phrases such as "history of SMM", "continues to be/still SMM", "previously SMM", "diagnosed with SMM in", or "had SMM since"
    - mention of SMM diagnosis followed by a date of diagnosis
    
Only mention of patient "with" or being "followed for" SMM does not qualify for a historical diagnosis.

Do not infer historical SMM from labs, imaging, bone marrow findings, M-protein level, or ICD code alone. Base your answer only on explicit textual evidence in the note.
    
Output "new" if there is no explicit evidence showing that the diagnosis is historical.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Provide your response as a single word: hx or new. Do not include any explanations, introductory text, or punctuation.
